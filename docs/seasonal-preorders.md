# Seasonal themes, preorders and research access

- **Admin → Brand Name:** select Original, Summer, Winter, Fall, Spring, Halloween, Black Friday, Christmas, New Year, Valentine’s Day, Easter or Store anniversary. Custom colors override the seasonal action colors. Ambient decorations respect reduced motion and never intercept clicks. Original removes seasonal effects.
- **Minimum visitor age:** select 18 or 21 in Brand Name. This is a store eligibility policy, not a statement about a universal legal age. Visitors must also meet higher local requirements. The first-visit gate remembers acceptance on that browser; increasing the age requires a new acceptance. Policies, support and unsubscribe remain accessible. This is self-declaration, not verified ID.
- **Admin → Products:** enable preorder for the entire product (inherited by every variant), or independently on selected variants. Set a future expected dispatch time and finite stock. While preorder is enabled, stock means available preorder slots, not physical inventory. Full payment is collected. Slot reservations use the existing atomic stock flow; unpaid expiry/cancellation restores them exactly once. Paid cancellations require the existing admin/refund process.
- On arrival, reconcile physical stock and disable preorder. An elapsed dispatch date stops new preorder purchases until the admin updates the date or disables preorder. Previously purchased dates remain immutable on order details and invoices. This feature does not introduce automated partial shipment tracking or actual refund transfers.
- Checkout requires research/age confirmation and explicit acceptance of preorder dates. If a date changes, the server rejects a stale checkout so the buyer can refresh and accept the new date.
- **Admin → Policies → Load recommended drafts:** replaces editor contents only. Complete every `[COMPLETE: …]` marker, check actual provider/retention/delivery practices, and save to publish. Existing policies are never overwritten by a migration. Markdown source drafts are in `docs/policies/`. These are templates, not a guarantee of enforceability; obtain local legal review before publication. Research-only wording does not remove statutory consumer rights.
- Test with `node tests/seasonal-preorder.mjs`. The test uses an isolated, in-memory PGlite database and fake providers. It does not send emails or transfer funds.

Policy reference material: [EU withdrawal rules](https://europa.eu/youreurope/citizens/consumers/shopping/returns/index_en.htm), [EU delivery rights](https://europa.eu/youreurope/citizens/consumers/shopping/shipping-delivery/index_en.htm), [European Commission data protection information](https://commission.europa.eu/law/law-topic/data-protection/information-individuals_en).

## Full seasonal storefront editions

Each edition includes an original vector hero scene, a matching heading treatment, surface texture, announcement bar, decorated navigation, interactive motif, product-card details, section dividers, empty-cart art, and checkout accents. Winter has alpine scenery and aurora; summer has waves; fall has copper trees; spring has botanical details; Halloween has a moonlit laboratory and cobweb; Christmas has an evergreen scene and ornaments; New Year and anniversary editions have skyline celebrations; Valentine’s has rose details; Easter has pastel botanical art; Black Friday uses an editorial ticket treatment. Theme selection does not create discounts or change product prices.

Brand Name offers **Subtle**, **Balanced**, and **Celebration** intensity; editable headline, description and announcement; an optional countdown; and start/end times entered in your browser’s local timezone and stored as UTC. Before the start or at/after the end, the storefront returns to Original while retaining your custom brand colors. Scheduling is resolved on read and live in open storefronts, without a cron dependency. Countdown expiration hides the timer; it does not modify a promotion.

Use the live **Desktop / Mobile** preview to inspect unsaved drafts. Preview intentionally ignores schedule timing to show the selected appearance. The iframe accepts same-origin messages only from its own parent, cannot place orders, and does not modify saved settings. A standalone read-only design explorer is available at `/theme-preview`.

Visitors can turn motion off in the footer. Device reduced-motion preferences and the store’s motion setting take precedence. The interactive navigation motif also responds to a click or tap without relying on hover. Decorative layers do not intercept purchases or product selection, and ambient counts are bounded (6/14/26). Countdown updates are isolated from product-card rendering. No new image or animation service is required.

Additional checks: `node tests/render-seasonal.mjs` statically renders every design with motion/intensity variants, countdown expiry, escaping, and reduced-motion preferences. `node tests/seasonal-preorder.mjs` covers persisted settings, schedules, migration replay, and preorder regression behavior. Neither test uses a browser or live providers.
