## cancellation and preorders

Contact us promptly to cancel an unfulfilled order. Preorders are paid in full and have a recorded expected dispatch date. A preorder is not a waiver of withdrawal rights. Do not send additional funds to an expired payment quote.

## consumer withdrawal

Where EU consumer distance-selling rules apply, you normally have 14 days from receipt to notify us of withdrawal without giving a reason, and a further 14 days after notification to return goods. For relevant split deliveries, the legal start date may be receipt of the last item. We provide the required pre-contract information and any mandatory online withdrawal facility. Contacting support remains an available withdrawal channel.

You can use this notice: “I give notice that I withdraw from the contract for [items], ordered on [date], received on [date]. Name: [name]. Address: [address]. Date: [date].” No reason is required. A written signature is needed only if sent on paper.

A legal exception applies only where the actual product and circumstances satisfy that exception and it was properly disclosed. We do **not** treat all chemicals, opened laboratory products, or research purchases as automatically exempt. Any deduction for diminished value must be lawful and supported, not a blanket restocking fee.

## damaged, incorrect, lost or defective goods

Report problems with your order number and evidence reasonably available. Keep packaging if safe. We investigate and provide applicable repair, replacement, price reduction or refund rights; we do not limit mandatory guarantee periods to a short reporting window. The seller remains responsible for consumer transit risk until lawful delivery. You are not required to recover the price from the carrier first.

## refund method and timing

For a valid consumer withdrawal, reimbursement is normally due within 14 days after notification; where legally allowed we may withhold until goods or proof of return are received. Standard outbound delivery charges are reimbursed as legally required; extra premium-service costs need not be reimbursed where the law permits. Other refunds follow the applicable statutory deadline and the confirmed remedy.

Refunds use the original payment method unless another method is expressly agreed without impermissible charges. Cryptocurrency refunds require careful confirmation of the supported network, asset and destination, and may need manual processing. We will explain the amount and conversion method and obtain agreement where required; we will not impose an unsupported network or a blanket “no crypto refunds” rule. An admin status marked “Refunded” alone is not proof that funds were transferred. We will provide transaction evidence or payment confirmation when the refund is actually sent.
