## preorders and mixed orders

Preorder stock represents a reserved allocation, not inventory ready for immediate dispatch. The expected dispatch date is displayed for the selected product/variant before checkout and recorded with your order. Payment is collected in full. Material delays will be communicated; contact support if the promised date is missed. Mixed orders may involve different dispatch dates and shipping origins. Confirm any essential delivery deadline with support before ordering. Any split shipment must be communicated with its own tracking information where available; we will not add unagreed shipping fees after purchase.

## tracking, failed delivery and loss

We provide available tracking after dispatch. Tracking can lag or contain errors and is not conclusive proof that goods reached you. Report missing, damaged, incorrect or stalled deliveries to [COMPLETE: support contact]. Provide your order number and relevant photographs if safe; do not open or return leaking or hazardous packages without safe instructions. Prompt reports help investigation but do not shorten statutory claim periods.

For consumer orders, transit risk normally remains with the seller until you or your nominated recipient receives the goods, subject to any lawful exception for a carrier independently commissioned by you. A carrier investigation does not remove our responsibility for non-delivery. We will provide replacement or refund remedies when legally due. We assess address errors and missed collection fairly and do not automatically forfeit payments.

## customs and safety

Import duties or clearance costs, if payable by you, will be disclosed where required and explained before purchase. We remain responsible for obligations the law places on the seller. Observe storage and handling instructions after receipt. Do not use these materials in people or animals. For an unsafe delivery, isolate the package only if safe and contact the appropriate emergency or hazardous-materials service when necessary.
